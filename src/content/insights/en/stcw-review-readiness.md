The IMO has undertaken a **comprehensive review of the STCW Convention and Code**. Administrations and training institutions that wait for the final text risk a compressed implementation timeline.

## What can be done now

- Review the effectiveness of the existing **quality standards system** for training, assessment and certification.
- Check that oversight of training institutions and simulators is documented and evidence-based.
- Map certificate recognition arrangements and their renewal dates.
- Identify where human-element topics — fatigue, leadership, wellbeing — are already addressed in training.

## Why it matters

A credible certification system protects seafarers' employability and the flag's reputation. Preparation now makes the transition to amended requirements orderly rather than reactive.
